## 2D avatar (sprite art)

The app is designed around an original 2D character: a scruffy cat girl. Its
art is being produced from [docs/avatar-brief.md](docs/avatar-brief.md). Until
it is added, the app ships without a built-in 2D avatar and starts with Feniel.

When the art is added, its license goes here. The recommended choice is
[CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/): anyone may
share and adapt the character with credit, but not sell it.

Credit is not required by CC0. It is given here anyway, with thanks.

## Animation clips (Kimodo)

The 27 clips in `modelli-3d/animations/` (`.vrma`: idle, greeting, sitting,
walking, dancing, being carried, the touch reactions…) were generated by Daku
locally with [kimodo.cpp](https://github.com/LocalAI-io/kimodo.cpp)
(Apache-2.0), a C++ port of NVIDIA Kimodo, from the text prompts in
`scripts/kimodo-series.json` and `scripts/kimodo-series-touch.json`, then
converted to `.vrma` by `scripts/kimodo-generate.mjs`.

- Motion model: **Kimodo SOMA RP v1.1** by NVIDIA, released under the
  [NVIDIA Open Model License](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/agreements/enterprise-software/nvidia-open-model-license/)
  (version of October 24, 2025), which allows commercial use and states:
  "NVIDIA claims no ownership rights in outputs". The model itself is not
  included here.
- Text encoder: derived from Meta Llama 3 and covered by the
  [Meta Llama 3 Community License](https://www.llama.com/llama3/license/).
  The clips contain no Llama material (only bone rotations), and the encoder
  is not included here. Built with Meta Llama 3.
- License of the clips: [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
  Use them as you like; credit to NVIDIA Kimodo is appreciated.

## Test motion data

`scripts/fixtures/kimodo-soma77-sit.bvh` is an excerpt (13 frames) of a
"sit down" motion generated by Daku with the NVIDIA Kimodo demo, on the SOMA
skeleton. It is used only by the tests, to check that real Kimodo exports are
converted correctly. Kimodo models are released under the NVIDIA Open Model
License.

## Avatars you import

Avatars imported through "Import avatar" stay on your computer, under your
user data folder. They are never uploaded anywhere. You are responsible for
respecting their licenses. Many VRM models declare their terms in their own
metadata. Check them before sharing screenshots or recordings.
